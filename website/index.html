    An open, standard-based, vendor-agnostic, end-to-end remediation process

    Financial industry grade standards. Bigger than OSERA.

    Patching is only half of the problem. Agreement on how any fix is produced, verified, and attested is equally fundamental to enable regulated consumption at scale. OSERA's financial-industry-backed remediation standards are the acceptance gate for OSERA releases, and the shared bar large institutions can use to consume packages from upstream and from commercial producers.

    Feeds and Advisories

    OpenVEX and CycloneDX feeds, so a release states what it fixes and scanners can read that statement.

    Fork Management

    How patched-source repositories, patch branches, and baseline tags are named and published.

    Patch Application

    How a patch feed is discovered and applied across a dependency estate.

    Recipient Evidence

    Concise guidance on what changed, and which surface a recipient should test.

    Release Process

    Version naming, approved producers, test reports, bytecode compatibility, and publication hygiene.

    Source Changes

    Whether a fix is an upstream backport, the link to that change, and license headers on new files.

    Get involved

    Three ways to get involved

    Incubated where the regulatory bar is highest, and open to institutions of every size, in every country, and to technology firms with upstream expertise. Three ways in.

    🏦

    Financial institutions

    Fund and govern as a Premier member, or consume the same attested releases as a General member. General fees run from $15k to $90k by firm size, so smaller and non-US institutions can take the same fix.

    Become an OSERA member
    🛠

    Patch and infrastructure vendors

    Commercial patch producers and supply-chain vendors join FINOS to conform to the Remediation Standards. FINOS members can also bid to maintain a line. No OSERA membership required.

    Become a FINOS member
    📦

    Build it yourself

    Every maintained line is public under the upstream licence. Build from the forks, and adopt the same open standard the exchange uses as its gate.

    Build from GitHub
